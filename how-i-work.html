How I Work

A practical six-step approach to getting work from problem to outcome.

01

Understand

Identify the real problem, stakeholders and desired outcome.

02

Structure

Break the work into requirements, responsibilities, timelines and dependencies.

03

Coordinate

Bring together the people, resources and information needed to move forward.

04

Adapt

Respond to resistance, constraints, new information and unexpected problems.

05

Execute

Follow through on procedures, responsibilities and deadlines.

06

Improve

Review what worked, identify gaps and strengthen the process.
