How I Work

Structure first. Adapt quickly. Execute carefully.

A practical working approach shaped by operational roles, coordination-heavy work and real-world constraints.

01

Understand

Clarify the real problem, stakeholders and desired outcome before jumping into solutions.

02

Structure

Break the work into requirements, responsibilities, timelines, dependencies and workable steps.

03

Coordinate

Bring together the people, resources and information needed to move the work forward.

04

Adapt

Respond to resistance, constraints, new information and unexpected problems without losing sight of the outcome.

05

Execute

Follow through on procedures, responsibilities and deadlines with attention to operational detail.

06

Improve

Review outcomes, identify gaps and strengthen the process for the next cycle.

In practice

The approach changes with the project.

The same principles show up differently depending on the problem: a campus transition needs dependencies and risk awareness; a process redesign needs adoption; a digital initiative needs clear requirements.

Operations

Make readiness visible.

Track what must be complete, who owns it and what could delay the operational handover.

Change

Bring people into the change.

Listen to concerns, explain the reason for change and build workable adoption rather than relying on instructions alone.

Requirements

Translate needs clearly.

Start from the user and business problem, then communicate the required structure to the people responsible for technical implementation.
