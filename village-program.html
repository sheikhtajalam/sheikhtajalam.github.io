Village Literary & Sports Development Program

A recurring community program combining volunteers, fundraising, competitions and long-term youth development.

Founder / Coordinator2021–Present~20 volunteers over time₹100/month contributionProgram Management

The problem

The program was founded to give village children greater exposure to literary and sporting competitions and help build confidence before larger examinations and competitions.

The program developed into a recurring community initiative combining monthly activities with an annual mega event.

My role

I founded the program and served as conductor, coordinator and budget manager while based in the village. I coordinated volunteers, managed contributions and supported recurring activities and the annual event.

Program model

Monthly contributions
Volunteer coordination
Literary activities
Sports activities
Annual mega event
Community support

Resources & funding

Participants contributed ₹100 per month through the program's finance structure. Funds supported awards, event expenses, materials and logistics. The team also met donors and community figures to seek external support when funding was short.

Over time, volunteers were recruited and trained, with the program continuing after my relocation from the village.

Challenges

Volunteer changes and recruitment created scheduling pressure and occasional gaps in content publishing and monthly activities. Funding shortages in 2023 and 2024 required additional donor and dignitary outreach.

Scale & outcomes

The program has involved approximately 20 volunteers over time, around 100 families, recurring competitions and an annual event covering football, Kabaddi, track and field, tug of war, quiz, poem recitation, speeches and spelling competitions.

What this demonstrates

Program management · Budget management · Volunteer coordination · Community stakeholders · Recurring delivery · Resource constraints · Sustainability
