Campus Relocation & Operational Transition

Leading the operational transition from a congested school facility to a standalone campus.

01 · Objective

What needed to change

Make the new standalone campus operationally ready while coordinating construction, procurement, installations and the transition from the previous facility.

02 · My ownership

Where I was accountable

Operational execution, coordination, procurement, progress monitoring and schedule adjustment. Acquisition and negotiation remained with the Managing Director.

03 · Outcome

What changed

The school transitioned into the standalone campus, with reopening adjusted by approximately 10 days because of project constraints.

The challenge

The school was operating from a congested campus accommodating both English- and Assamese-medium sections. A standalone campus was acquired, but making it operational required construction supervision, room allocation, installations, procurement and coordination with the workforce.

The challenge was not simply moving facilities; the new campus had to become ready for normal school operations while managing construction dependencies, weather and resource constraints.

My role

As Principal, I led the operational execution required to prepare the new campus. My responsibilities included construction supervision, room allocation, electrical and CCTV installation follow-up, raw-material procurement, coordination with construction crews, progress monitoring and schedule adjustments.
Ownership boundary: The Managing Director handled campus acquisition and negotiation; I led the operational execution required to make the campus ready for use.

Project approach

Assess requirements
Plan campus setup
Coordinate work
Procure materials
Monitor progress
Adapt schedule
Prepare transition
Resume operations

Challenges & schedule impact

Risk / constraint: Rain and waterlogging affected construction activities, alongside minor financial constraints. The vacation period had to be extended from 20 to 30 days, and regular classes resumed approximately 10 days later than originally intended.
ChallengeEffect

Outcome

The school transitioned from the previous congested facility into the new standalone campus after construction and operational preparation. The project reinforced that operational readiness—not simply completion of individual tasks—is the real endpoint of a transition project.

What this demonstrates

Coordination · Procurement · Risk awareness · Schedule management · Stakeholder communication · Operational readiness
