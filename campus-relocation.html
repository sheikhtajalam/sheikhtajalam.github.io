Campus Relocation & Operational Transition

Leading the transition from a congested school facility to a standalone campus.

Principal / Project Lead~2 months285 students22 teachersOperations

The challenge

The school was operating from a congested campus accommodating both English- and Assamese-medium sections. A standalone campus was acquired, but making it operational required construction supervision, room allocation, installations, procurement and coordination with the workforce.

The challenge was not simply moving buildings; the new campus had to become ready for normal school operations while managing construction dependencies, weather and resource constraints.

My role

As Principal, I supervised the operational execution required to prepare the new campus.

My responsibilities included construction supervision, room allocation, electrical and CCTV installation follow-up, raw-material procurement, coordination with construction crews, progress monitoring and schedule adjustments.

Ownership boundary: The Managing Director handled campus acquisition and negotiation; I led the operational execution required to make the campus ready for use.

Project approach

Assess requirements
Plan campus setup
Coordinate work
Procure materials
Monitor progress
Adapt schedule
Prepare transition
Resume operations

Challenges & schedule impact

Rain and waterlogging affected construction activities, alongside minor financial constraints. The vacation period had to be extended from 20 to 30 days, and regular classes resumed approximately 10 days later than originally intended.

ChallengeEffect
Rain & waterloggingDisrupted construction activities.
Resource constraintsAdded pressure to execution and procurement.
Schedule slippageRequired an extended vacation and adjusted reopening.

Outcome

The school transitioned from the previous congested facility into the new standalone campus after construction and operational preparation. The project reinforced that operational readiness—not simply completion of individual tasks—is the real endpoint of a transition project.

What this demonstrates

Coordination · Procurement · Risk awareness · Schedule management · Stakeholder communication · Operational readiness
