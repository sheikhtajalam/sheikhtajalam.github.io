Career Story

From customer operations to project leadership.

Different environments, recurring themes: problem solving, coordination, process discipline and execution.

I have worked across customer operations, education, community programs and organizational leadership. Each role added another piece of the same skill set: understanding problems, coordinating people, following processes and adapting when circumstances change.

My current role as Principal brings those experiences together at organizational scale. Project Management is the natural next step — formalizing and extending the way I already approach complex work.
