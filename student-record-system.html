Student Comprehensive Record System

Designing a continuous student-information system to replace fragmented academic records.

Project Lead / Requirements Owner<1 month initial rollout285 students22 teachersDigital phase

The problem

The school had examination records but no comprehensive, ongoing system for capturing the broader picture of student development. Examination papers were generally stored after assessment, creating limited opportunity for continuous feedback.

The project aimed to create a structured record covering academic, attendance, behavioural and other student-development information so teachers and parents could use it as part of an ongoing feedback loop.

My role

I designed the information framework and requirements for the system, introduced it to teachers and coordinated its implementation. I then communicated the school’s business requirements to an external developer for the digital application.

Role clarity: I was the business/user-side requirements owner. The external developer handled the technical application build.

Implementation

The paper-based system was introduced through a staff meeting, orientation and walkthrough, and implemented in under one month before the first terminal examination. All 22 teachers and 285 students were covered by the system.

Digital transition

The next phase involved an external developer translating the paper structure into application tabs and fields. The transition was approached progressively so the existing paper process could provide continuity while the digital system was developed.

Paper system → parallel transition → digital system

Outcome

The project created a structured, continuous student-record process and established the requirements foundation for digital implementation. The key achievement was turning a fragmented record-keeping problem into a defined business process and requirements set.
